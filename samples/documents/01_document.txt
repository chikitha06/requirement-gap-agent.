Campus Lost-and-Found App — Proposal

Problem
Students often lose water bottles, ID-card holders, books, and other items around campus. They do not know where to ask whether an item was found. The app is intended for students and campus staff.

Features
Users can post a lost item. Users can post a found item. Users can search posts by category and location.

Technology
The frontend will use React. The backend will use FastAPI and SQLite.

Testing
We will test the login page and item search manually.
